# NYNA — Brand Kit

Brand assets for NYNA (nyna.co.uk). Use these for anything that represents the brand —
social profiles, decks, documents, ads.

## Brand guidelines

- **`NYNA-Brand-Guidelines.pdf`** — 7-page A4 guide: brand essence, logo & clearspace,
  colour palette, type system, the wave motif, and voice. Share this with any designer
  or collaborator before they produce NYNA-branded work.

## Colour

| Token | Hex | Role |
|-------|-----|------|
| Ink | `#0D1116` | Deepest background |
| Surface | `#181D24` | Primary surface (dark) |
| Accent / Sky | `#5ECFF9` | Single accent — use sparingly |
| Light | `#F2F2F2` | Text / contrast on dark |
| Paper (light mode) | `#ECEFF3` | Light background |
| Ink (light mode) | `#161B22` | Text on light |
| Accent (light mode) | `#0C8FCB` | Accent on light |

Dark is primary. One accent only — never multi-hue gradients.

## Typography

- **Unbounded** (Medium) — display & the NYNA wordmark. Google Fonts.
- **Satoshi** (Bold / Medium) — headings & UI. Fontshare.
- **DM Sans** (Regular / Medium) — body & captions. Google Fonts.

## Logo

Wordmark set in Unbounded Medium with light letter-spacing. Keep clearspace equal to the
height of the “N” on all sides. Don’t stretch, recolour, rotate, or add effects.
