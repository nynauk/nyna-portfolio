## Posting

- **Size.** Instagram shows a post 1080 wide and scales a larger file down itself, so the 4K files
  can be posted as they are.
- **HDR.** Each JPEG holds an ordinary picture and, after it, a map of where the light is. An app
  that understands it shows the lamp, the lit copper and the LEDs brighter than white. To keep it,
  the file has to arrive untouched: move it to the phone as a file (cable, cloud drive, AirDrop)
  and post it from the phone's gallery. Most chat apps recompress pictures and drop the map, as
  does a screenshot. Where HDR is not supported the same file is simply an ordinary JPEG.
- **LinkedIn** has no HDR and shows the ordinary picture. `linkedin-banner.png` is made at twice
  the size LinkedIn shows.
- **Stories.** The story versions keep everything clear of the name at the top and the reply bar
  at the bottom.
- **Video.** 60 frames a second, no sound. The post video begins and ends on its still, so it
  loops, and its first frame is its cover. The videos are standard dynamic range.
